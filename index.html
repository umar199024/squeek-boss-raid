How the raid works

  1. One boss a week. It arrives Monday 00:00 UTC with a fixed HP and leaves the next Monday. Defeat it before then or it escapes.
  2. Only the hamster hits. Every wheel pass SqueekCore certifies on Sepolia is one point of damage. Nobody can buy, fake or speed up a hit, and his wheel time is never forced: naps are part of the game.
  3. Raiders earn. Enlisted raiders get 1 assist point per 10 passes in every round after they joined.
  4. Call the rounds. Each 10-minute SqueekCore round is a Nap (0), Jab (1–399) or Smash (400+). Correct calls score 10 / 100 / 120, and each one in a row adds +10% (up to +100%).
  5. Classes.
  6. Victory. Everyone enlisted before the killing round gets +500. A correct call on the killing round earns Last Hit +250.
